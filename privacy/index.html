Clueday

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

Clueday is a daily puzzle app for iPhone, made by John White ("we"). This policy explains how Clueday handles your information.

Stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, streaks and hint use are stored locally on your iPhone so the app and its widget can show them. They are not sent to us.

Usage analytics (RevenueCat)

Clueday uses RevenueCat, Inc. solely for usage analytics, so we can count new and active users. RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous customer ID generated by the app (not your name, email or Apple ID);
  • device and app information, such as app version, operating-system version, device model and locale;
  • app-open activity.

RevenueCat processes this information under its own privacy policy.

What Clueday does not do

Clueday has no accounts, purchases or ads. It does not track you, does not access your location and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Deleting your data

Deleting Clueday from your iPhone removes the data stored on your device.

Apple

When you download Clueday from the App Store, Apple may collect information independently under its own privacy policy. We do not receive personal data from Apple about you.

Children

Clueday is suitable for all ages. It has no accounts and asks for no personal details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

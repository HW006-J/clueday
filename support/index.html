How does Clueday work?

There is one puzzle each day, the same for everyone. It might be a cryptic crossword clue, a quick clue, a general knowledge question or a brain teaser. You have three guesses, and one optional hint if you get stuck. Solve it to earn a Gold or Silver result.

When does the new puzzle arrive?

Every day at 8am UK time (Europe/London).

How do I add the Lock Screen widget?

  1. Touch and hold your Lock Screen, then tap Customise.
  2. Choose Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the widget area above or below the clock.
  4. Find Clueday and choose a widget (rectangular, circular or inline).
  5. Tap Done.

Tapping the widget opens that day's puzzle.

How do streaks work?

Solve the daily puzzle on consecutive days to build your streak. Missing a day ends it.

What does the hint do?

Each puzzle has one optional hint to nudge you towards the answer. Using it can affect your result.

How do I reset my data?

Your progress is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it. Clueday has no accounts, so we cannot restore lost progress.
